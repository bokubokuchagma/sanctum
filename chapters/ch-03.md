# Chapter 3: The Flame of Ruin

The Mountaintops of the Giants were unforgiving. Howling blizzards bit through the Tarnished's armor, chilling them to the bone. Yet, the bitter cold was soon replaced by an unbearable, suffocating heat.

There, guarding the Forge of the Giants, stood the last of his kind. The Fire Giant, a colossal monstrosity of braided red hair and molten rock, roared to the heavens. In his hands, he wielded a massive metal plate, using it like a devastating plow to shatter the snowy peaks.

The Tarnished drew their weapon, the blade glowing with enchantments. To burn the impenetrable thorns of the Erdtree, the giant had to fall, and the Flame of Ruin had to be unleashed.
