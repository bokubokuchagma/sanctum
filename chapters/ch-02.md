# Chapter 2: The Glintstone Academy

The misty lakes of Liurnia stretched endlessly, a flooded graveyard of a once-great kingdom. Rising from the center of the fog was the Academy of Raya Lucaria, a gothic monument to the study of primeval glintstone magic.

The Tarnished trudged through the shallow, marshy waters, their boots heavy with mud and the blood of albinaurics. Above, the sky shifted into an unnatural, starry dusk—the lingering magic of the Carian Royals. 

Within those towering spires, Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon, cradled the amber egg. To mend the Elden Ring, her Great Rune was required, even if it meant tearing through centuries of forbidden sorcery.
