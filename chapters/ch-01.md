# Chapter 1: The Tarnished Returns

The Erdtree loomed on the horizon, a monolithic beacon of golden light that cast long, shimmering shadows across the shattered landscape of Limgrave. The air was thick with the scent of ozone and decaying leaves. 

A lone figure, cloaked in tattered armor, stepped out from the cool darkness of the Stranded Graveyard. The Tarnished had returned. Grace, long lost to their kind, now guided their path as a faint, golden wisp dancing in the wind.

Before them stretched a world broken by the Shattering. To the north, the storm-veiled castle of Godrick the Grafted perched upon the cliffs, a testament to the mad ambitions of the demigods. The Tarnished tightened their grip on their rusted longsword. The journey to the Elden Throne would be paved with blood and grace.
