The stone was cold against their cheek. For how long they had lain there, suspended in the dark between life and death, they did not know. The air was still, heavy with the scent of dust and dried blood. 

They opened their eyes. The Chapel of Anticipation was silent.

Rising to their feet, the Tarnished felt the familiar, agonizing weight of their armor. They were one of the exiled, driven from the Lands Between long ago by the decree of Queen Marika. They had fought, died, and rotted in the badlands beyond the fog. Yet, here they were. A faint, golden thread of light—grace, a force they had been stripped of centuries ago—shimmered in the air, pointing toward the heavy wooden doors.

Pushing the doors open, the wind howled. A chasm stretched below, and across a precarious rope bridge lay the ruins of a fortress. But there was no time to admire the grim architecture. From the shadows above dropped a monstrosity—a creature of stitched flesh, wielding multiple grafted swords, screaming a corrupted battle cry.

The Tarnished raised their rusted blade, but they were weak, their muscles atrophied by death. The Grafted Scion struck with a fury of unnatural limbs. Steel met flesh, and the world faded to black.

***

"Don't worry, Torrent. They are the one."

A soft, feminine voice cut through the darkness. The Tarnished blinked, water dripping onto their face. They were lying in a flooded cavern, the Stranded Graveyard. A cloaked figure with a spectral steed had been watching them, but as the Tarnished shifted, she vanished into the mist.

Determined, they pushed themselves up. The cavern was a labyrinth of forgotten heroes and restless spirits. They navigated the dark, their sword feeling a little lighter in their hands, their senses sharpening with every step. The golden grace was stronger here, practically pulling them toward a pair of massive stone doors.

They pushed against the stone, the doors grinding open to reveal blinding, radiant light. 

The Tarnished stepped out onto the cliffs of Limgrave. The sight took their breath away. The Erdtree, a colossal, impossible structure of pure golden light, dominated the sky, its shimmering leaves falling like slow rain across the shattered landscape. Below, ruins of churches and castles dotted the verdant plains, patrolled by mad soldiers who had lost their minds to the Shattering.

To the north, the storm-veiled castle of Godrick the Grafted perched upon the cliffs, a testament to the mad ambitions of the demigods. The Tarnished tightened their grip on their rusted longsword. They had returned to claim the Elden Ring, and the journey would be paved with blood.
