Before the golden leaves of the Erdtree cast their luminescence across the Lands Between, the world belonged to blood, stone, and the primordial Crucible. In those untamed years, there were no thrones of polished marble, nor songs of divine grace. There was only strength.

And in the desolate Badlands, strength bore a single name: **Hoarah Loux**.

He was a warrior of primeval fury, chieftain of an unruly clan whose only law was survival. With bare hands calloused by iron and bone, he shattered stone and felled beasts twice his stature. He did not seek worship. He did not ask for dominion. His breath was the wind through the crags, and his battle roar was the storm that split the heavens.

Yet from the golden capital of Leyndell, the newly risen god Queen Marika turned her gaze toward him. The Golden Order was young and surrounded on all sides by ancient rivals: the ancient dragons of Farum Azula, the fire worshippers of the craggy peaks, and the wild lords of the tempest. Marika required a sword. She required a champion whose vigor could bend kingdoms to her will.

She chose Hoarah Loux to be her consort—and the first to ever bear the title of **Elden Lord**.

***

"A monarch cannot be a beast," Marika whispered into the cold halls of the sanctum, where the scent of cedar and myrrh struggled against the iron smell of blood that clung to the chieftain's flesh. "A lord must command reverence, not terror. Thou must govern thine own savagery, or it shall consume the kingdom before it is built."

To take the throne, the savage chieftain made a terrible vow. 

Upon his broad shoulders was placed **Serosh**, the aged and noble Lord of Beasts. A colossal white lion with eyes like molten amber and claws ancient as bedrock, Serosh sank his claws directly into the flesh of the chieftain’s back. It was not a decoration; it was a living harness.

As the beast regent took hold, an immense weight settled into Hoarah Loux's soul. The boiling, tempestuous fury in his veins grew calm. The mindless thirst to tear flesh with bare fingers was subdued, trapped beneath the weight of regal duty.

The chieftain discarded his tribal rags for robes embroidered with golden filigree. In his hands, he took up a heavy, broad-bladed greataxe—a weapon forged to cleave armies in twain, yet balanced for a king.

Hoarah Loux died in that ceremony. 

In his place stood **Godfrey**, the First Elden Lord.

***

> *"Long ago, the beast regent Serosh was grafted upon the back of Godfrey to quell his boundless battle rage. Only when the lord became one with the beast could he conduct himself as a king."*

Standing upon the ramparts of Leyndell for the first time, Godfrey looked out upon the Lands Between. The Great Erdtree rose behind him like an incandescent pillar, showering gold upon his heavy shoulders. Serosh growled low in his ear, an ancient warning of the blood that was yet to come.

Godfrey gripped the haft of his greataxe until the golden metal groaned under his knuckles.

"Let the enemies of the Erdtree take heed," Godfrey murmured, his voice rumbling like an earthquake beneath the stone. "The Age of the Erdtree hath begun."
