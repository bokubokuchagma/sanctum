# Chapter 4: The Crumbling City

Transported beyond the flow of time, the Tarnished awoke in Farum Azula. The ancient city hung suspended in the sky, slowly being torn apart by eternal, swirling tornados.

Lightning crackled across the ruins, illuminating the ancient dragons that soared through the tempest. This was the resting place of Maliketh, the Black Blade, the shadow of Queen Marika who guarded the Rune of Death. 

Every step across the floating debris was treacherous. The air was heavy with the weight of ancient sins. Here, the final piece of the puzzle awaited. Death had to be unbound to truly kill a god.
