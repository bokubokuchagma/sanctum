# Chapter 5: The Elden Lord

The Erdtree was burning, its golden leaves reduced to raining ash. Inside its hollow core, the final battle awaited. The physical form of Radagon had crumbled, giving way to something far more ancient and terrifying.

The Elden Beast emerged from a pool of cosmic water, a translucent, ethereal leviathan with starry wings that mirrored the galaxy itself. It was the living incarnation of the Golden Order, divine and unforgiving.

The Tarnished stood their ground, raising their weapon one last time. This was the end of an era. The Shattering was over, and a new Elden Lord was about to be crowned.
